# Foodpanda Knowledge Base

## Company Overview
Foodpanda (stylized lowercase) is a Singapore-headquartered online food and grocery delivery platform, founded in 2012, and owned by Berlin-based Delivery Hero. It is Delivery Hero's lead brand across Asia, operating in 11+ markets including Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Cambodia, and Laos. Services include food delivery, pickup, and **Pandamart** (quick-commerce/grocery delivery).

## Corporate Mission Statement
> "Bringing good food into your everyday" — official mission line used on Foodpanda's LinkedIn company page.

## Campaign Tagline
**"Make Life Delicious"** — active tagline currently used across Foodpanda Philippines TikTok/Instagram campaign captions (e.g. restaurant partner launches, festive promos). Confirmed as a live, currently-used hashtag/tagline (#MakeLifeDelicious), not a legacy one.

## Brand Personality
- Friendly, fun, youthful, modern, customer-focused
- Speaks like a helpful friend who already knows what you're craving
- Uses the panda mascot/persona playfully (puns, panda-themed emoji)

## Target Audience
- Students
- Office workers / busy professionals
- Families
- Urban, mobile-first consumers who order on impulse (cravings-driven)

## Regional Hashtag Pattern
Foodpanda consistently uses a **country-code suffix pattern** for region-specific hashtags:
`#foodpandaPH`, `#foodpandaSG`, `#foodpandaTH`, `#foodpandaPK`, `#foodpandaMY`

## LinkedIn-Specific Tone (different from consumer platforms)
LinkedIn content is corporate/employer-branding, not promotional. Common themes:
- Career fairs and hiring ("#LifeAtfoodpanda")
- Company culture and employee celebrations
- Leadership recognition / industry rankings
- Regional milestones and growth stats

Example real post pattern (paraphrased, not verbatim): a LinkedIn post thanking a university's career fair attendees, tagging the event and school, ending with a careers link and 2-3 hashtags combining company + event tags.

## TikTok-Specific Content Themes (from research)
- Rider/delivery culture (day-in-the-life, city tours by delivery riders)
- Comedy/reaction skits about hunger and cravings
- Restaurant partner spotlights and limited-time discount promos
- Tutorial-style content ("how to set up your foodpanda account")
- Heavy use of trending sounds/templates (e.g. CapCut templates) rather than original audio

## Known Real Campaign Example (Philippines, 2026)
"Andok's Returns to Foodpanda Philippines" — restaurant partner comeback campaign.
Pattern used: Hook (celebratory emoji) → what's back → discount window (e.g. time-boxed % off) → tagged partner handles → hashtags (#AndoksOnfoodpanda #MakeLifeDelicious #foodpandaph).
This is a strong real-world template for "restaurant partner spotlight" post types.
