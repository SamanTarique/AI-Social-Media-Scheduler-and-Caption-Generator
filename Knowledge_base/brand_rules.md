# Foodpanda Brand Rules

## Universal Rules (all platforms)
1. Always include the base brand tag `#foodpanda`, plus the relevant regional tag (e.g. `#foodpandaPK`) when posting for a specific market.
2. Never guarantee delivery times ("guaranteed in 20 minutes") — this is a real operational/legal risk area for delivery brands and should always be avoided.
3. Never make negative or comparative claims about competitors (other delivery apps).
4. Keep the panda persona playful but never juvenile to the point of undermining trust — humor should feel confident, not silly-for-its-own-sake.
5. Every consumer-facing post (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X) needs a clear call-to-action. LinkedIn posts are the one exception (see below).
6. Emojis: 1-3 per post on Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/X. Zero to 1 on LinkedIn.

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram
- Tone: playful, visual-first, short captions (1-3 sentences).
- Structure: Hook → short context → CTA → hashtags.
- Hashtags: cap effective use at 2-3 tags (Instagram's algorithm and best-practice research both point to diminishing returns beyond this).
- Always pair with a strong visual; caption should complement, not repeat, the image.

### Facebook
- Tone: similar playfulness to Instagram, but slightly more informational — state the actual offer/detail (e.g. discount %, time window) rather than relying purely on a hook.
- Slightly longer captions are acceptable (2-4 sentences).

### TikTok
- Tone: casual, meme-literate, first-person or POV framing works well.
- Captions are secondary to video/audio — keep them very short, almost caption-fragment style.
- Lean into real recurring themes found in research: rider/delivery culture, hunger-relatable humor, restaurant partner spotlights, quick tutorials.
- Use `#fyp` and `#FoodTok` alongside brand tags for discovery.

### X / Twitter
- Tone: brief, reactive, conversational — closer to a real-time customer conversation than a polished ad.
- Good for engagement/poll-style posts ("rate your order 1-10") and quick promo call-outs.
- Avoid long hashtag strings; 1-2 tags max fits the platform's brevity norm.

### LinkedIn — Different Rules (Important)
LinkedIn is **not** a promotional/discount channel for Foodpanda based on research — real posts are corporate and employer-branding focused.
- Tone: professional, warm, achievement- and culture-oriented. No discount codes, no "order now" CTAs.
- Topics: hiring/career fairs, company culture, leadership recognition, regional growth milestones.
- Structure: context/thanks → what happened → forward-looking or careers-link CTA → 2-3 hashtags (commonly `#LifeAtfoodpanda` plus an event/region tag).
- Do not reuse Instagram/TikTok captions here — they will feel off-brand and overly casual for LinkedIn's audience (recruiters, partners, industry peers).

### Pinterest
- No confirmed official Foodpanda presence was found during research. Treat any Pinterest content as a **pilot/optional** channel.
- If used: lean on general food-Pinterest conventions — save-oriented captions, recipe/comfort-food framing, minimal hashtags (1-2), since Pinterest search behaves more like a search engine than a social feed.

## Common CTAs (research-confirmed, consumer platforms only)
- "Order Now"
- "Sign Up"
- "Start Exploring"
- "Drop your answer" / "Comment below" (for engagement posts)

## Emoji Style Guide
Frequently observed: 🐼 🍔 🍕 ⚽ 🔥 🎉 ❤️ 📈
Use sparingly and only where they reinforce the message (e.g. 🐼 for brand moments, 🔥 for urgency/hot deals, 🎉 for celebrations/launches).
